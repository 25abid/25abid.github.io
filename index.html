Sometimes, I spend my time learning things :/

I’m Abdulkader. I study geospatial data science at the University of Toronto, with minors in applied statistics and computer science. A lot of my work ends up being something visual: a map, a drawing app, or a game. This site is where I put those projects and write about what I’m learning along the way.

What I’m looking into

Lately, I’ve been looking into building native Windows apps. I’d like to make something that solves a problem students run into and eventually put it on the Microsoft Store. I’m still figuring out what that first app should be.

Off the clock

I play a lot of video games and sometimes record my gameplay for my YouTube channel. This is an ongoing side quest that I plan to spend more time on. I enjoy content creation, and it gives me a chance to explore different ideas, challenge my creativity, and develop my editing skills.
